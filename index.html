KRA calendar

Dates that cost money if you miss them.

Late filing and late payment both attract penalties and interest. These are KRA's standard due dates.

9thPAYE

Every month, for the previous month's payroll.

20thVAT

Every month, for the previous month.

20thInstalment tax

4th, 6th, 9th and 12th month of your year.

30JunAnnual returns

Individual income tax returns.

If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, check iTax or ask us. Your own obligations depend on how you're registered.
